Fractional CTO

A CTO in the room — part-time, and on the hook.

For founders and CEOs who need senior engineering leadership now — not a nine-month search. I own the technical direction, the team, and the outcomes, at the fraction you actually need.

What I own

  • Technical strategy & architecture. The roadmap, the hard build-vs-buy calls, and the direction the whole team builds toward.
  • The engineering team. Hiring, structure, mentoring, and the standards that make delivery predictable instead of heroic.
  • Delivery & velocity. Shipping on a cadence your customers and investors can count on.
  • Security & reliability posture. The risks that stay invisible until they become existential.
  • The board conversation. Translating engineering into terms your directors and investors can act on.
  • AI as an advantage, not a liability. Using modern tooling to move fast — without building something no one on the team can maintain.

When a fractional CTO fits

  • You have engineers, but no senior leader to point them.
  • You're between CTOs and can't afford the gap.
  • You're a technical founder who needs to get back to product and customers.
  • You're a portfolio company that needs a steady technical hand after investment.

How it works

An ongoing engagement, structured around what you need — typically a set commitment each week, fully remote. I'm accountable, not advisory: in the room, owning outcomes. Terms by conversation.
