## Lab Notes by email

When you sign up, I keep your email address, the sign-up wording you agreed to, and when you confirmed. I use them only to send you Lab Notes. You can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email. To have your record deleted entirely, email me.

Sign-ups that are never confirmed are deleted after 7 days.

The list is stored with Cloudflare, and the emails are sent through Resend from its EU region. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a sign-up comes from a person. Open and click tracking are off.

## On your device

If you sign up, your browser remembers that you did, so the sign-up box says "You're on the list" instead of asking again. It keeps one word on this device ("pending" until you confirm, then "subscribed"), never your email address, and it's never sent to the site. Unsubscribing clears it, and so does clearing this site's data in your browser.

## Analytics

The site counts visits with PostHog's EU cloud. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, so there's no consent banner.
